Highlights

1

There's no longer a best model, only the best model for a shape of work.

Nate B Jones sorts work into forest, forest-and-trees and trees problems. Independent tests found OpenAI's new Astra flat on coding but leading science, 3-D and robotics: a spike most reviewers couldn't see from where they stood. Audit the task, chain two models, and test outside your own lane.

2

The gap at the top is small, and the premium doesn't last.

Suhail Doshi, who sells cheaper open-model tokens, found a cheap open model matched a top model on his own design tasks at about a thirtieth of the cost. Keep a small eval folder built from your own work. Models also do better working on files directly than through an app.

3

A new front door opened, and every business started from zero.

ChatGPT's business profiles launched in beta on 10 November. Local listings are US-only for now, and business agents are open first to online sellers. Phil Risher's moves for the small player: other ponds, real content, past customers and referral partners.

4

What nobody can flatten: a customer made to feel like a VIP.

Bing Gordon named Amazon Prime and spent a decade on Amazon's board. Add emotion to the economics, offer a second currency, build values into the daily routine, and push from good to great when everyone else stops.
